03 · About

About me

I'm an AI engineer with an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (SEECS) at NUST, Islamabad. My work spans the full ML loop: preparing messy real-world data (DICOM scans, satellite tiles, benchmark tables), training and fine-tuning models, and checking the results with care.

I'm most interested in applied AI where accuracy matters, especially healthcare and language models. I like projects that end in something people can use, whether that's a leaderboard submission, a GeoTIFF map or a Flask demo.
